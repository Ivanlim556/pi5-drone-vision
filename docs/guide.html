The rule that keeps it safe The Pi reports obstacles; the flight controller decides and flies. The Pi never sends stick commands, and the RC link never goes through the Pi — lose the Pi and you lose avoidance and video, never the aircraft. Each phase adds one new thing, so a failure points at its own cause.

The easy way through ad1–ad6 — what to touch, and what the meter should say

Everything goes through J1, the 22-pin ribbon connector on the board. It uses the standard Raspberry Pi camera pinout. Find pin 1 by the small triangle, dot or "1" printed beside J1; pin 22 is at the far end. An easier ground point is J2 pin 3 on the small 3-pin connector.

J1 pinWhat it is
22+3.3 V in — the only power pin
1, 4, 7, 10, 13, 16, 19GND — any of them
2–3, 5–6, 8–9, 11–12, 14–15Camera data pairs — don't probe these with power on
17EN_RAW — camera enable
18FLASH — the one to cover with Kapton
20 / 21SCL / SDA (I²C)

J1's contacts are tiny: touch the solder joints where each contact meets the board (back edge of the connector), not inside the slot. A sharp probe tip and a magnifier help. Unsure which end is pin 1? Beep from J2 pin 3 (GND) along J1 — the GND pins (1, 4, 7 …) beep, and the pattern shows the direction.

Easier test points — from the board's own netlist Each rail has a big 10 µF capacitor (0805, the largest caps on the board) from the rail to GND. Measure across its two ends: C24 = +3.3 V in (same net as J1 pin 22) · C21 = +2.8 V · C22 = +1.8 V (beside the two regulator chips) · C23 = +1.2 V (bottom-left, near L1). Fixed GND point: J2 pin 3 (J2 pin 1 = FLASH, pin 2 = TRIGGER). Board off: none of the four may read 0–2 Ω. Board powered (ad8): 3.3 / 2.8 / 1.8 / 1.2 V across the same caps.
Seen on the board photos, 2026-10-05 The sensor is bare: the M12 lens needs an M12 lens holder screwed through the two holes beside it — the lens does not screw into the board. Frozen by this fabrication run

TRIGGER has no pull-down on these boards Ball D5 (→ J2 pin 2) floats on every board in this batch. Master mode, the default, ignores it. external-trigger and sync-sink are off-limits: a floating input gives random exposures that look like a software bug. Fix if needed: a 0402 10 kΩ from J2 pin 2 to J2 pin 3 (GND).
Also committed 0.20 mm drills ×134 — JLCPCB's 4-layer minimum.
MIPI traces 0.120 mm where 100 Ω wants 0.127 mm; pairs matched under 0.10 mm, so impedance is the one open variable if the link fails only at high rate.
R7/R8 fitted — 2.7 kΩ I²C pull-ups in parallel with the Pi's (~1.2 kΩ combined). Legal at 400 kHz; suspect them if the bus misbehaves.
